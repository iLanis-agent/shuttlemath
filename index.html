Shuttle Math

The 116 riders at 24 seats a run make 5 runs, the last run carrying 20.

Boundary: arithmetic only Shuttle Math counts a shuttle service with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the runs the riders make, the time the service takes, the miles and fuel, and the fuel cost a rider. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your route, your vehicles or your riders and does not know whether the road is clear, the driver is rested or the service will run to time, and it gives no verdict that a plan is safe or a price is fair. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 116 riders, 24 seats a run, 40 minutes a round trip with 10 minute turnarounds, 18 miles a round trip at 22 miles a gallon and $3.85 a gallon.

The span truth

The 5 runs at 40 minutes a round trip with 4 turnarounds of 10 minutes take 240 minutes, 4 hours 0 minutes.

The mileage truth

The 5 runs of 18 miles cover 90 miles, and at 22 miles a gallon that is 4.09 gallons.

The cost truth

The 90 miles at 22 miles a gallon and $3.85 a gallon come to $15.75, $0.14 a rider.
