Shuttle Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts a shuttle service with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the runs the riders make, the time the service takes, the miles and fuel, and the fuel cost a rider. It uses no named constants. The riders, the seats, the round trip minutes and miles, the turnaround, the miles a gallon and the fuel price are yours to decide and read off your own plan and pump. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your route, your vehicles or your riders and does not know whether the road is clear, the driver is rested or the service will run to time, and it gives no verdict that a plan is safe or a price is fair. Every number here is your own entry.

The runs truth

Runs: the riders divided by the seats a run, rounded up, with the size of the last run.

The span truth

Span: the runs times the round trip minutes, plus a turnaround between each pair of runs.

The mileage truth

Mileage: the runs times the round trip miles, and the gallons at your miles a gallon, to two decimal places, half rounded up.

The cost truth

Cost: the miles at your miles a gallon and fuel price, to the cent, half rounded up, and spread over the riders.
